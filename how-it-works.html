How FSM Works End-to-End
From Work Order Intake to Digital Proof

A connected four-stage operational lifecycle that coordinates clients, dispatchers, field technicians, and enterprise finance in real-time.

END-TO-END WORKFLOW

From Service Request to Successful Completion

Manage every stage of the field-service lifecycle through a connected, workflow-driven process.

01

Raise Service Ticket

Create a service request with customer, service, priority, and destination details.

02

Assign Field technicians

Review the ticket and assign the appropriate field technician member.

03

Start Field Work

The technician accesses assigned tickets through the native support application and begins the service activity.

04

Monitor SLA

Track remaining SLA time and ticket priority throughout the service process.

05

Track Service Location

Use destination information and location-aware functionality to support field execution.

06

Complete Service

Perform the required installation, maintenance, or repair activity.

07

Capture Digital Signature

Obtain customer confirmation as proof of service completion.

08

Close & Notify

Close the completed ticket and notify relevant stakeholders through email and in-application notifications.
