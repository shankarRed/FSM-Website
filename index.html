Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started. Helping teams move forward with confidence.

What capabilities does the FSM platform provide?

FSM provides a centralized platform to manage service tickets, technicians, field operations, SLA monitoring, workflows, location tracking, service verification, and reporting.

Which types of service businesses can benefit from FSM?

FSM is designed for independent service providers, multi-brand service companies, brand-authorized service centres, installation and maintenance contractors, and commercial, industrial, and IT equipment service providers.

How does multi-tenant data management work?

FSM allows multiple service providers to operate on a shared platform while maintaining separate business data. Each provider can manage its own employees, customers, services, technicians, SLAs, and service tickets.

How does FSM help assign the right technician?

FSM supports technician assignment based on relevant skills, availability, and assignment requirements. This helps Service Managers identify suitable technicians for service requests.

How does FSM monitor SLA performance?

FSM provides SLA-related visibility through ticket priorities and live SLA countdowns. Support users can monitor ticket urgency, while managers can track SLA compliance and service performance.
